# ootto.ai – business model notes

> Source: public web-search snippets gathered on 2026-09-28 (the site itself is blocked from
> this environment, so nothing below was read from the live page). Treat as a starting point
> to be verified against the real site once it is reachable; the full extraction (offer,

## Positioning
- "The most proficient AI operator for Instagram": a 24/7 AI operator that grows an Instagram
  account, engages every follower and turns comments into qualified leads.
- Onboarding: you give Ootto your website; it reads the business, the audience and what works for
  similar brands; it produces up to 7 reels a week which you approve by swiping right (or swipe
  left to request changes); approved reels are posted on schedule.
- Broader "AI autopilot" narrative: connects business tools once and runs invoicing, lead
  follow-up and reporting automatically.
- Open-source / community wedge: GitHub org `Ootto-AI` publishes free Claude skills
  (`ootto-watch`, `claude-invoice-skills`), and "every Ootto tool also works inside your own Claude".

## Revenue model
- Trial-to-subscription: $1 today, then $48 / month (full service).
- Free open-source skills as lead generation for the paid operator.
