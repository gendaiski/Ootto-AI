FAQ

Questions people ask before the first swipe.

Does Ootto post without my approval?
No. Every reel waits in your queue until you swipe right. Swipe left and Ootto redrafts it with your note. Replies to comments run continuously in the voice it learned from your site, and you can tighten that voice at any time.
What does "up to 7 reels a week" actually mean?
Ootto drafts one reel per day at most. You choose how many to approve; approved reels are scheduled into the slots that perform best for your audience.
How does a comment become a lead?
Ootto answers every comment. When someone shows buying intent, it moves the conversation into DMs, asks a couple of qualifying questions, and creates a lead card with what it learned. You only see the ones worth your time.
What happens after the $1 trial?
You pay $1 today and the full service continues at $48 a month. Cancel anytime from the dashboard. Trial length assumed
Do I need Ootto if I already use Claude?
Every Ootto tool also works inside your own Claude as a free open-source skill. The paid operator is for people who want it running 24/7, connected to Instagram and their tools, without driving it themselves.
